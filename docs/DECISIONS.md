# Decisions

Choices the spec left open. Add a line whenever you pick the simplest option: the date, your role, the decision and why.

- **Branches:** finished work goes to `dev`, and only dtri moves `dev` to `main`, once the whole team has checked it. Nobody else pushes to `main`.
- **Pre-push hook:** `npm install` adds a hook on each person's machine. It runs `npm run check` before a push to `dev` and refuses pushes to `main`, because GitHub can't protect branches on this plan.
- **`src/core/` belongs to Role 3 (dtri),** by the team lead's decision. Requests to change it go through dtri. `src/core/balance.json` is open to everyone.
- **Main menu, content warning, Esc menu and settings are Role 1's,** built in `src/builder/`. Core holds plumbing only, with no game screens.
- **Pre-launch screen and the return and first-step cinematics are Role 4's,** because they tie into the story and the Adrift ending.
- **`ShipSpec` gaps:** `parts` have `x, y, z` so radial symmetry can be expressed, nozzles are only `sl` or `vac`, and `launch: { tempC }` carries the weather for the Cold Morning ending. See `src/core/contract.js`.
- **`flight:telemetry`** was added so the black box can record peak values, and `builder:highlightPart` was added to the event list.
- **Δv per leg (2026-11-07, Role 2, measured with the real flight physics):** `balance.json` `deltaVKmS` is now ascent 4.1, transfer 1.0, landing 0.3, return 2.7 km/s. Ascent and landing are measured: a good gravity turn to a 95 km orbit costs about 4.1 km/s (1/10-size planets with real gravity and real air, like Kerbal's 3.4 km/s), and a chute plus a last-moment retro burn on Mars about 0.25–0.3 km/s. Return is a Mars ascent (measured 1.7) plus the transfer. The true transfer for this scale is about 2.05 km/s, but then no one-stage ship can reach Mars: the core Reliable mock has 5.74 km/s in total (its `stats.deltaV` of 8900 doesn't match its masses and Isp), and 4.1 + 2.05 + 0.3 is 6.45. So the transfer is compressed to 1.0 km/s, the same way the spec compresses distance and time. With it, the Reliable mock reaches Mars and lands with about 0.3 km/s spare when flown well. Revisit if staging or Mars refuelling arrives.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: builder balanced against Role 2's measured legs** (one-way 5.4 km/s, round trip 8.1). The builder's `LOW_DV` warning fires below the one-way total; the Formula Lens shows both totals. The large turbopump is 1,000 kN in `src/builder/data/engines.json` so a 5.7 km/s single-stage ship still has TWR ≥ 1.2 off the pad (Role 2's overheating rates are keyed by pump letter, not thrust, so nothing changes for them). The "Reliable" preset has about 5.6 km/s.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: insolvency.** `balance.insolvencyFloor` ($1.0M, about the cheapest ship that can leave the pad). When funds are below it the hangar shows "DECLARE BANKRUPTCY", which emits `ending:show { endingId: 'bankrupt' }` and requests `ENDING`. Role 4: on that ending please reset money even though it is still above $0 (the contract's "money < 0" alone would leave the player stuck forever).
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: launch windows.** The player picks a launch slot in the hangar (`src/builder/data/launch.json`): the soonest is free and cold (2 °C, the Challenger setup), later ones cost pad fees. The fee is charged with the ship at confirm and counts for `OVER_BUDGET`; `ShipSpec.launch.tempC` carries the slot's temperature.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: cheap seals are a discount part** (−$100k, reliability 0.85, `coldRatedSeals: false`), not a required choice: tanks come cold-rated unless the player takes the money.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: builder data shapes.** The rocket is an attachment tree (`src/builder/README-ARCH.md`); parts and all text live in `src/builder/data/*.json`; the ShipSpec adds `stats.ascentCdA` and `stats.cdA` for Role 2. Money is spent only at the Go/No-Go confirm. Legs attach to the engine's radial node so the feet reach the ground.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: `src/builder/index.js` also exports `openPauseMenu()` and `openSettings()`** so other roles can open the Esc menu and settings from their states. Settings keys in the save: `sensitivity`, `invertY`, `quality`, `volume`, `skipSensitive`.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: the hangar is a walkable engineering bay.** `HANGAR` is a round integration hall in first person (`src/builder/bay/`): one machine per job (integration stand, propulsion, structures, crew systems, warehouse, test stand, range, handbook), the vehicle on a hold-down stand with a service tower, a wall screen fed by a camera (render-to-texture, refreshed only when the vehicle changes). It has its own small first-person controller because Role 2's `WalkController` is internal to `src/surface/`. CC0 Poly Haven assets (HDRI, PBR sets, five props, 18 MB) live in `public/assets/polyhaven/` and are credited in `CREDITS.md`. dtri: the vehicle still uses your `rocketPart:` art; an art pass there now shows up in the bay, on the wall screen and on the catalogue cards (which are rendered from the same meshes).
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: real hardware for the builder parts.** Twenty-two catalogue parts use public-domain NASA models from `github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources` (`public/assets/nasa/`, 45 MB, Draco-compressed, decoder in `public/assets/draco/`): a Space Shuttle main engine, the ESAS crew module, the Stardust capsule, the Shuttle RCS pod, Satellite Kit solar wings and antennas, the ORU box and the docking tunnel. Tanks, nose cones, heat shields and fins are detailed procedural hardware so the stack snaps cleanly (`src/builder/bay/partMeshes.js`). The pictures on the cards are rendered from the same models. More NASA models (Robonaut, suits, Ingenuity, RASSOR, CubeSats, Mobile Launcher, and a reserve: ISS, Mir, Skylab, Canadarm, crawler, gantry, probes, tools) stand in the bay as real equipment; the engineer's body is Khronos' CesiumMan (CC BY).
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: the vehicle design page replaces the hologram.** A design desk opens a full-screen engineering drawing of the stack, top to bottom, modelled on NASA's Dawn launch-vehicle diagram (`src/builder/designPage.js`): every part has a label, clicking one shows a row of replacement cards (picture, role, pros/cons, mass, price) and the part's **material** options (`src/builder/data/materials.json`: structure alloys, engine chamber metals, equipment grades, each with mass/cost factors and a reliability delta that `stats.js` applies). An empty stand shows HEAD / BODY / TAIL and walks the player down from the nose. The hologram and its drag-and-drop were removed: too many buttons, and the pictures were too small to compare.
- **2026-11-07, Role 1: Vite 8 needs Node 20.19+ or 22.12+.** Node 21 fails before `vite` starts (`util.styleText`). dtri: consider adding `"engines": { "node": ">=22.12" }` to `package.json` and a line in the README.
- **Audio director (2026-11-07, Role 3):** `src/audio/director.js` (`initAudioDirector()`, called once from `src/core/main.js` boot) plays the ambience from `state:changed` and `eva:oxygen`, so no other role calls it. Flight keeps `engine.rumble`, `setAir` and its alarms; the surface module keeps its own loops and its single `alarm.o2` beep as O₂ crosses 30/15/5 %, so the director only repeats `alarm.o2` every 6 s (30 % or less) or 2 s (15 % or less, with `suit.breath` at rate 1.35) and never beeps on the crossing itself. ENDING stops everything. `audio.play` gained `fadeIn` and the new `audio.fadeTo(id, volume, seconds)` lets loops fade instead of cutting.
- **Cockpit screens show telemetry (2026-11-07, Role 3):** the three screens redraw from `flight:telemetry`, at most 4 times a second and only when values changed. The telemetry has no O₂ field and no fuel capacity, so O₂ shows `--` and the fuel bar is a share of the highest `fuelKg` seen this flight. Max-Q uses the `maxQ` field if sent, else 40 kPa (the flight model's limit).
- **Automatic resolution (2026-11-07, Role 3):** `graphics.setAutoResolution(bool)` (default on) and `graphics.pixelRatioNow`. Average frame time above 20 ms for 2 s lowers the pixel ratio one step (0.1 × the quality's ratio, floor 0.6 ×); under 13 ms for 6 s raises it. On a 60 Hz screen frames never go under 13 ms, so a held 60 fps (under 18 ms) also raises one step after 30 s; each raise that has to be undone within 15 s doubles that wait (up to 8 min), so it settles instead of pumping. The `?debug=1` panel has a Low/High choice saved with `save.update({ quality })`, used after a reload.
- **Cockpit layout (2026-11-07, Role 3):** the window sill is at y = -0.42 and the top at y = 0.8 (about -17 to +30 degrees from the eye; the game's flight camera is 65 degrees vertical). The 3 instrument screens, their tilted face and the side lamps sit under -38 degrees, so they are out of the default view and the HTML HUD sits over plain matte dash; the player looks down with the mouse to read them. `vfx:entryPlasma` is 1.5 m tall to reach the new sill.
- **Launch warm-up and part art hook (2026-11-07, Role 3):** entering LAUNCH used to stall 100–170 ms because the cockpit's and the flame's lights change the light count, so three.js recompiled the whole launch site on the first frame (and again on pressing C). `src/graphics/warmup.js` compiles both camera views against Role 2's prebuilt launch site while PRELAUNCH is idle, then removes its temporary objects; the worst frame entering LAUNCH is now about 33 ms on an M5. It never touches a scene that is on screen. Built-in `rocketPart:` objects are now clones of one template per type (shared geometry and materials). `assets.setRocketPartArt(fn)` lets Role 1's hangar models replace them in flight. The cockpit screens show fuel against the ship's real tank (`stats.fuelMass`) and an engine heat bar instead of the O₂ bar flight never fed. The `?debug=1` panel starts collapsed in the bottom-right corner.
- **Rover is a Blender model (2026-11-07, Role 3):** `assets.get('rover')` serves `public/assets/models/rover.glb` (about 48 k triangles, about 2.1 MB with a baked AO map), built by `scripts/blender/rover.py`; regenerate with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/blender/rover.py -- --out public/assets/models/rover.glb [--shots DIR]` (Blender 5.2). The GLB starts loading when `src/graphics/rover.js` is imported and `assets.preload` waits for it; until it has loaded (or if it is missing) `createRover()` returns the old code-built rover, which is kept as the fallback. The interface for Role 2 is unchanged (pivots `wheelFL/FR/RL/RR`, first child spins, eye now (-0.32, 1.45, -0.45)).
- **Second rover, Earth views, terrain and cockpit (2026-11-07, Role 3):** `rover:lrv` is a Blender model of the Apollo LRV (`scripts/blender/lrv.py` → `public/assets/models/lrv.glb`, regenerate with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/blender/lrv.py -- --out public/assets/models/lrv.glb`); its fenders are children of the wheel pivots after the spin node, so they steer but never spin, and `roverLrv.js` forces the spin node to index 0 after loading. Both rover build scripts check numerically that no tyre comes within 2 cm of the body over ±21° of steering (Role 2's full lock is atan(2.29 / 6.2) ≈ 20.3°). The planet shader now computes the exact sphere hit per pixel, adds procedural land, ocean, cloud and city-light detail below about 60 km, and `skyState` in `shared.js` fades the stars in daylight. Mars terrain (`terrain.js`, `groundTier.js`) has about 480 craters, a river channel into the delta, buttes, dunes and a crater-rim range, all inside `getTerrainHeight` so walking and driving match what is drawn; the landing zone stays flat within 60 m and crater-free within 150 m, and the Halcyon wreck sits on a levelled 40 m bench. The launch site (`earthLand.js`, `launchSite.js`) has hills, horizon mountains and about 3,000 instanced trees that hide above 9 km. The cockpit interior lives in `cockpitParts.js`; `createCockpit` never throws (it falls back to an empty interior). The sandbox walking views have a fly mode (F).
- **Cabin redesign and cockpit cache (2026-11-07, Role 3):** the cabin moved to `src/graphics/cabin.js` + `cabinParts.js` (about 79 k triangles, 4 lights as before, station and prop coordinates unchanged; the old `createCabin` in `interiors.js` is no longer registered). The cockpit is cloned from one template built in idle time after boot (later builds about 1.5 ms), and the PRELAUNCH warm-up also compiles the pad view with both the cockpit and the engine flame (4 point lights), which removed the 150 ms LAUNCH hitch the new interior had caused. The Reliable mock has `armorHits: 1` so one debris hit doesn't end the demo run.
- **Testing money (2026-11-07, Role 3, asked by dtri):** testing kept running out of money, so in development (`npm run dev`) and with `?debug=1` every page load starts with $1,000,000,000 (and `economy.reset()` returns to it). `?money=N` sets another amount there, e.g. `?money=5000000` to test the real economy or `?money=900000` to test bankruptcy. `npm run build` ignores both: the shipped game starts with `balance.startMoney` ($5M) and keeps the save.
