# Launch pad section: assets

No asset files are used or downloaded by this section.

- The pad, towers, flame trench, mobile launcher platform, water tower, lightning towers, slidewire baskets, storage
  spheres, tanker truck and weather station are procedural three.js geometry written in `art.js`.
- The ground under each launch site is a texture drawn at runtime on a canvas in `art.js` (no image file).
- NASA 3D Resources models already in `public/assets/nasa/` (`mobile_launcher.glb`, `mobile_launcher_pad.glb`,
  `gantry.glb`, `crawler.glb`) were inspected but not used (see NOTES.md). Nothing to add to CREDITS.md.
