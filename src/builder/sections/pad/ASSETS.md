# Launch pad section: assets

No asset files are used or downloaded by this section.

- The pads of all ten complexes are procedural three.js geometry written in `art.js`: launch mounts (slab, Starbase-style
  raised table, Gagarin's flame pit with its support arms, flame trench, mobile launch platform), towers (strongback,
  fixed umbilical tower, integration tower with catch arms, rotating service structure), hold-down clamps and arms,
  water ring, rainbirds and water tower, lightning mast and towers, crew arm and slidewire baskets, storage spheres,
  chilling plant, tanker truck, weather station, radar, field mills and a windsock.
- The ground under each complex is a texture drawn at runtime on a canvas in `art.js` (no image file).
- The card pictures are the same art rendered small by `partIcons.js` (`objectIcon`), nothing baked.
- NASA 3D Resources models already in `public/assets/nasa/` (`mobile_launcher.glb`, `mobile_launcher_pad.glb`,
  `gantry.glb`, `crawler.glb`) were inspected but not used (see NOTES.md of the earlier wave). Nothing to add to CREDITS.md.
